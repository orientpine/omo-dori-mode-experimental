# Rollover: moving the lead or a lane to a new session

A session that runs for a long time grows its context, compacts it again and again, and can get worse at its work, or get stuck repeating one failed fix. A rollover moves the work to a fresh session: `dori handover` for the lead, `dori handover --lane <key>` for a lane. The lead decides each rollover and the owner is told afterwards (the owner's decision, 2026-10-12: the lead handles rollovers and only reports that it did). Dori measures, proposes, and logs who decided, until the owner turns the automation on.

## The rule: a rollover needs a decision on record

- `dori handover` and `dori handover --lane <key>` refuse a real run without `--approved-by <ref>` (`--dry-run` needs none). The usual ref is the lead's decision, `lead:<reason and the signal behind it>` (`lead:STUCK rung 4 on loss, research and challenge did not move it`): one that names no signal (a STUCK rung, an owner-check finding, a measured threshold) is refused, and so is one when the handover has no way to tell the owner afterwards (`DORI_DISCORD_TOKEN` and `DORI_DISCORD_CHANNEL`, or a `discord:<id>` lane thread). When the owner asked for the move (a new model for a lane: "open this one with fable high"), there is no signal to name: the ref is then `lead:<reason>, owner <message id>` or `lead:<reason>, owner <link to the message>` (`lead:형 지시로 모델 변경, owner 1560876422535577662`; `형` works for `owner`). That message is looked up in the inbox: it must be there, written by `DORI_DISCORD_OWNER` or `DORI_KAKAO_OWNER_ID`, and in the link's channel when a link is given; a made-up id, another author's message (a pair bot, a watched bot), a link into another channel, or no owner id set up is refused, and a cited id that does not hold refuses even next to a real signal. A reason is still needed (`lead:owner <id>` alone is refused), the rest of the handover's checks are the same, and it is still the lead's decision: the owner is told afterwards. Never put a signal in the ref that the lane did not show to get past this check (10-17: the lead refused to write "compactions 4, age 37.5 h" to move E7 to the model the owner had asked for; this path was added instead). After the move the handover posts `handover.reportText` to the owner (the lane's thread, else the channel) and logs `decidedBy: "lead"`, the reason and that message's id (`ownerReport`); a message that fails is `ROLLOVER_OWNER_REPORT_MISSING` in the watch log and `ownerReport.error` in the log, and the lead tells the owner by hand. The owner may still decide one themselves: the ref is then a question card `Qn` that the owner answered with its first option (the card must exist, be the one `dori rollover-propose` posted for this lane or the lead, as the rollover log records, and be answered with that option, so a yes on another lane's card or on any other question moves nothing), or a link to the owner's Discord message (`https://discord.com/channels/<guild>/<channel>/<message>`). A link is the lead's own statement and is not fetched: use it only when the owner said yes outside a card. Free words are refused. A card about an earlier session of the lane (another pane) does not count either. The ref goes into the note, the lane history and the rollover log.
- `auto:handover.autoRolloverLanes` (lanes) and `auto:handover.autoRolloverLead` (the lead), exactly these, are accepted only while `handover.autoRolloverLanes` (for lanes) or `handover.autoRolloverLead` (for the lead) is on. Both are off by default. Turn one on only after the owner looked at `dori rollover-stats` and said so. With a key on, `freshness` starts the handover itself when a measured threshold is crossed or, for a lane, at rung 4 of the stuck ladder (`ROLLOVER_AUTO`); the handover still refuses while a local chain would die, a background eval cell still runs, a turn runs or a question is open, and it never moves a lane that runs or watches a training or eval chain (a tag in `handover.trainingTags`, or a live monitor matching `handover.trainingWatch`, as e7-beyond-eth's `q267_chain_watch.sh` for a run on another node): that is refused once, the pane gets no more automatic starts, and the lead moves the lane by hand (owner's rule, 2026-10-19). The owner gets `handover.reportText` only after a move that happened (a refusal is in the watch log). An automatic lane start (`dori handover --lane <key> --wait-idle <handover.autoWaitMin>`) moves the lane at its idle moment, not on a clock: it holds the lane's lock, tries at once, and while only a running turn or an open question holds the lane it watches the pane every 3 s for up to `handover.autoWaitMin` (20) minutes (`HANDOVER_WAIT_IDLE`); the moment the turn ends it freezes the old agent before the new session opens (`HANDOVER_FROZEN ... at its idle moment`), so no goal continuation, nudge or message starts work the note would not carry, and a turn that slipped in thaws it and the wait goes on. A lane that stayed busy that long (`blocked: "busy"` in the rollover log), or whose local chain or background eval cell would die (`blocked: "work"`, never killed), gets one `[LEAD]` request to reach a safe point, write its conversation summary and run `dori handover ready <key> --note <file>` (again after `handover.autoRetryMin` (30) minutes while it is still busy); freshness then starts the move again: at once after a busy refusal, and after a work refusal when the lane marked ready, when the chain pids it named ended, or after `autoRetryMin`. There is no last try (owner Q284, 2026-10-19: the old `handover.autoMaxTries` gave up after 6 starts, twice for yoodori-gate-gaps): a move that failed or rolled back is started again after `autoRetryMin`, doubling up to 8 times that while failures go on. Zombies (`[bash] <defunct>`, ended processes the agent has not collected) are no chain: they refused 5 automatic moves of yoodori-gate-gaps on 10-19. A session that was due while the key was off (only `ROLLOVER_DUE` so far) gets its first start at the next sweep after the key is turned on and `freshness` restarted. `freshness` prints `ROLLOVER_AUTO_READY` (both sides and their thresholds) or `ROLLOVER_AUTO_OFF` when it starts.
- Never move a real lane or the lead without that approval, not even to test. Tests run on a scratch lane (`dori launch` one, `dori pause` it so freshness leaves it alone, close it afterwards).

## The flow

1. A stuck lane reaches rung 4 of the stuck ladder (a failure after rung 3's research and challenge, one hypothesis tried `stuck.fixatedAfter` times after the lane branched, or an owner-check finding still there after the re-read the lead confirmed): freshness prints `STUCK <key> "<metric>" rung 4 ... [ROLLOVER_DUE]`, once per rung. `dori freshness` also reads each working (or not-done: it keeps working) lane's and the lead's measured signals (context use from the status line, compactions and age from the session log); when one crosses its threshold (all off by default) it prints `ROLLOVER_DUE <key|lead> <what crossed> in <pane>`, once per pane. Paused lanes are skipped.
2. The lead runs `dori rollover-propose <key|lead>`. It runs a dry-run handover (the note the new session would get, and what blocks the move now) and prints `ROLLOVER_PROPOSAL` (`handover.proposalText` with the signals, the lane's stuck rung and the note) for the lead to decide; the proposal goes into `<stateDir>/rollover-log.jsonl`. `--dry-run` prints it without logging.
3. When the lead decides yes: for a lane, ask it to reach a safe point first (`[LEAD] reach a safe point, write your conversation summary (dori handover ready --template prints its headings) to a file, then run dori handover ready <key> --note <file>`), then run `dori handover --lane <key> --approved-by "lead:<reason and signal>"`. For the lead, write the summary first (`dori handover --template` prints its headings), then `dori handover --approved-by "lead:<reason and signal>" --note <file>`; an automatic lead rollover has no summary, so the new lead is checked against the records and the owner's recent messages only. The owner gets the one-line report when it is done.
4. `dori rollover-stats` sums the log up: signal values at each proposal, the time to approval, each step's time and result, who decided and whether the owner was told, and each lane's watch-log quality signals (NUDGED, NO-REPORT, STUCK, OWNER_CHECK findings, NOT_SUBMITTED, LANE_BLOCKED, and SAME_FIX_3, REPEAT_SUSPECT and INTENT_DRIFT from older logs) in the 24 hours before and after (`--window H`). The automation thresholds are set from this.

## When a session counts as long

Measured on 2026-10-11 over 183 local session logs (27,652 assistant turns): 15 lead sessions and 75 registered lane sessions, against the quality labels the records hold (owner corrections in lead sessions, `[LEAD] not done` and other rejections in lanes, done objections, tool errors, SAME_FIX / INTENT_DRIFT / NUDGED lines in the watch log, and errors in the 10 turns after each compaction compared with the 10 before).

**What the data shows: no length at which quality measurably drops.**

- Lanes: all 7 rejections came in the first 6 hours and before any compaction. Error rates do not rise with context use, compactions, age or turns. The longest lane session (E7: 3,777 turns, 45.8 h, 12 compactions) has no rejection.
- Lead: 2 owner corrections in total, both in one short session (turns 72 and 103). The longest lead session (3,888 turns, 85 h, 7 compactions) has none.
- Around compactions: 0 corrections in the 10 turns before or after any of 34 compactions; 1 tool error after, 0 before.
- Every candidate cutoff (context 40/60/80 %, 1/2/4 compactions, 6/24/48 h) fires mostly on sessions that later show no problem: 71-100 % of its alerts. The labels are few, so this does not prove long sessions are safe either.

For comparison, these length cutoffs would have asked only the longest sessions, about the top 5 % in the measured logs. They are not the defaults; see the decision below.

| Side | Context use | Compactions | Age | Sessions that would have been asked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lane | >= 80 % | >= 4 | >= 24 h | 6 by context, 4 by compactions, 4 by age, of 75 |
| Lead | >= 80 % | >= 4 | >= 48 h | 2 by context, 1 by compactions, 3 by age, of 15 |

### What published work shows

Searched on 2026-10-11 by eight research lanes (benchmarks, vendor docs, tool defaults, compaction studies, long-horizon studies, loop detection, practitioner reports, and one lane that only attacked the claims), about 150 searches. Every source below was read, not taken from a search snippet; no browser was attached, so pages that need one (Fiction.LiveBench, Reddit) are marked secondary or left out. No source measures a rollover threshold for claude-opus-5-5, or compares continuing a coding session with a fresh one at matched state.

| Candidate signal | Evidence | Rating | Setting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Context use (%) | Benchmarks show real loss with length, but where it starts depends on model, thinking mode and task; vendor and tool triggers are overflow and cost reserves, not quality points; frontier 1M models differ widely; the 40-60 % rule is one practitioner's workflow | weak (근거 약함) | 0 (off) |
| Compactions | Repeated compression loses rules in a narrow lab setup and the first action after a compaction errs more; coding-task studies find no significant solve-rate drop from summaries; vendors call repeated compaction safe; the 2-3 count comes from user reports | weak (근거 약함) | 0 (off) |
| Session age (h) | Nothing isolates elapsed time; drift follows the history's content, not its length or age; the best models ran a simulated year without degradation | none (근거 없음) | 0 (off) |
| Same failed fix repeated | Vendor guidance (reset after two failed corrections), three tools' default limit of 3, and a controlled study where a fresh restart after about the 3rd failed debugging attempt helped every model tested; in-session correction also works, so it is a point to offer a reset, not a rule to force one | strong as a heuristic (근거 있음) | lanes: 3 |

The sources, with their conditions and numbers:

- **Length.** NoLiMa ([arxiv 2502.05167](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.05167v1), 2025-02, latent-association retrieval 1-32K): effective length 8K for GPT-4o, 4K for Claude 3.5 Sonnet (85.4 -> 29.8 %), 2K for Gemini 1.5 Pro, while literal matching stays at 98 %. Chroma Context Rot ([trychroma.com](https://www.trychroma.com/research/context-rot), 2025-07, LongMemEval, ~300 tokens vs ~113K with the needed facts kept): Opus 4 92 -> 38 % without thinking but 95 -> 72 % with it; Gemini 2.5 Pro thinking 96 -> 90 %. GPT-5.4 ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-4/), 2026-03, MRCR v2 8-needle): 86.0 % at 64-128K, 79.3 % at 128-256K, 57.5 % at 256-512K, 36.6 % at 512K-1M. Opus 4.6 on the same test: 76 % at 1M ([anthropic.com](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-6), 2026-02), 78.3 % at GA ([claude.com](https://claude.com/blog/1m-context-ga), 2026-03); Gemini 3.1 Pro 26.3 % at 1M ([model card](https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-3-1-pro/)). Coding: LongCodeBench ([arxiv 2505.07897](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.07897v1)) Claude 3.5 Sonnet issue fixes 29 % at 32K -> 3 % at 256K, Gemini 2.5 Pro 24 % at 256K -> 7 % at 1M, but code QA flat (GPT-4.1 72.6 -> 80.0 %). Counter: Michelangelo ([arxiv 2409.12640](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.12640v1)) finds no further loss from 128K to 1M after an early drop; HELMET ([arxiv 2410.02694](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.02694v1)) finds the loss task-dependent (reranking falls, RAG and citation stay flat) and needle tests correlate at most .8 with real tasks. And cutting context can hurt more than length: removing 75 % of a BABILong context naively costs Opus 4.7 .433, while removing only distractors gains .008 ([arxiv 2608.03297](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03297v1), 2026-08).
- **Vendor and tool triggers.** Claude Code compacts native 1M models at about 967K tokens ([model-config](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/model-config#default-auto-compact-thresholds)); Codex at 90 % of the window (244,800 of 272,000; [source @9f3d255](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/9f3d25588b3f0bdf452d4eda919f2c2365a89bba/codex-rs/protocol/src/openai_models.rs#L460-L537)); Cline 0.9 of usable input; OpenCode 10 % buffer; Amp 90 %; Goose min(80 %, 225K), capped below a price tier; Gemini CLI 50 % (20 % in older versions); Continue 121K of 200K. All of them are about overflow or cost, and they range from 50 to 97 %. Anthropic's API compaction trigger defaults to 150,000 tokens ([docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/compaction-threshold)). OpenAI says compaction is "safe to run repeatedly in long sessions" ([GPT-5.2 guide](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/gpt-5/gpt-5-2_prompting_guide)). Anthropic dropped context resets entirely once Opus 4.5 arrived and ran one continuous session with compaction; resets had been essential for Sonnet 4.5 ([harness design, 2026-03-24](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/harness-design-long-running-apps)).
- **Compaction quality.** Compaction Cliff ([arxiv 2608.22752](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.22752), 2026-08): Sonnet 4.6 keeps .53 of an agent configuration's rules after one round of summarization and .10 after five, at 50 % budget each round, with no new work in between. Its released code runs a custom "compress the configuration" prompt through the SDK, not a live `/compact`, and its pinned-rules arm (.96) carries different state between rounds than the plain arm, so the two are not a matched comparison. Lost in Compaction ([arxiv 2608.11242](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.11242v1)): rules on how the work must be done survive generic summaries 17 % of the time, over 90 % with a separate constraint extractor. TRACE ([arxiv 2608.06503](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06503v1), 2026-08, AppWorld, 2-16K budgets): the first action after a compaction is blocked or wrong +0.108 more often. Counter, on real coding: SWE-agent on SWE-bench Verified with summaries or masking, up to 250 turns, shows no significant solve-rate loss (Qwen3-Coder 54.8 vs 53.8 %; [arxiv 2508.21433](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.21433v2)); OpenHands' condenser solves 54 % vs 53 % without it ([blog, 2025-04](https://hub.openhands.dev/blog/openhands-context-condensensation-for-more-efficient-ai-agents)); in a harness study the gain from context management shrinks from 35.7 to 2.7 points as the window grows from 32K to 128K, so it mostly prevents overflow ([arxiv 2609.20804](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20804)). The count rules ("0-2 compactions excellent, 9+ degraded", [claude-code #33212](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/33212); "forgets the plan after 2") are single-user reports. A fresh session built from the same lossy summary inherits the same gaps, so what the note carries matters more than the count.
- **Age and many turns.** Vending-Bench ([arxiv 2502.15840](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.15840v1)): days until sales stop correlate r = 0.167 with days until the memory fills; Vending-Bench 2 ([andonlabs.com](https://andonlabs.com/evals/vending-bench-2), 2025-11) reports the best models running a simulated year "with no signs of performance degradation". Goal Drift ([arxiv 2505.02709](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.02709v1)): replacing the history with length-matched filler removes most drift, so the history's pattern drives it, not its length. Multi-turn underspecification costs 39 % on average already from two turns ([arxiv 2505.06120](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.06120v1)), which is about anchoring on early wrong answers, not age. METR: "Time horizon is not the length of time AIs can work independently" ([note, 2026-01](https://metr.org/notes/2026-01-22-time-horizon-limitations/)).
- **Repeated failed fixes.** Claude Code: "After two failed corrections, `/clear` and write a better initial prompt" ([best practices](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/best-practices)). Cline and Roo default to 3 consecutive mistakes; OpenHands nudges at the 3rd identical action and error and stops at the 4th; Gemini CLI nudges once on a loop, then aborts. Debugging decay ([Scientific Reports 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-27846-5), 18 models, HumanEval): fixing ability mostly collapses after the 3rd attempt, and restarting fresh at the same attempt budget improved all 6 models tested (e.g. 72.6 -> 82.8 %). Models condition on their own earlier errors beyond any length effect, though thinking models resisted it ([arxiv 2509.09677](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09677)). Counter: in a production A/B test, guidance appended to the running session also cut failures ([arxiv 2602.17037](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.17037v2)). And the new session should get what the failed attempts ruled out, not the failed command itself: replaying it verbatim makes repeats more likely ([arxiv 2608.23651](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23651)).

### The decision

- 2026-10-16, the owner (Q190, after the lead measured keeping a compacted session and moving to a fresh one at about the same cost per turn, 0.133 vs 0.135): automatic rollover on, on quality signals only: stuck rung 4, and a session that still carries 300k tokens or more right after its last compaction (`handover.laneAfterCompactK` / `leadAfterCompactK` = 300); context %, compaction count and age off. The decision below stands for hosts that have not made one.

- Lanes: rung 4 of the stuck ladder (replaced `handover.laneRepeats` = 3 on 2026-10-12, with the owner's approval). The 3rd failed attempt on one metric is rung 3: ulw research and a challenge of the next plan in the same session, because a lane that is researching cannot be moved and a moved lane would have to re-read the research. A rollover is due only when that still fails, when one hypothesis is tried 3 times after the lane branched (it is fixated), or when an owner-check finding is still there after the re-read the lead confirmed. The lead decides; often the better answer is a changed hypothesis in the same session (`dori fix-attempt` asks for three).
- Context use, compactions and age: 0 (off) for lanes and the lead. The evidence does not give a number that transfers to claude-opus-5-5 at 1M, and the local logs show no onset. `dori rollover-propose` still works by hand at any time, for example when the owner sees a session go wrong, and it logs the signal values either way.
- The handover note matters more than the timing: see "Upsides kept, downsides removed" below.
- Re-decide from `dori rollover-stats` after the first rollovers: the quality lines in the 24 hours before and after each one, and the owner's yes/no on the proposals. The missing evidence is a matched comparison on this model: the same checkpoint continued vs moved to a fresh session. The automation keys stay off until then.

The full research record (sources ledger, claim graph, each lane's report) is kept outside the repository, under `~/work/.omo/ulw-research/20261011-211925/` on the host where it was made.

## Rollover or compaction only

The owner asked whether Dori could do without rollovers and rely on auto-compaction alone. Researched on 2026-10-11 by a second team (compaction-only runs, fresh-session costs, handoff contents, handoff checks, practitioner reports, two lanes measuring our own logs, and one lane attacking the claims). Record: `~/work/.omo/ulw-research/20261011-215413/`.

**What supports compaction only.**
- Vendors run long work on compaction without resets. Anthropic dropped context resets once Opus 4.5 came: one continuous session, the SDK compacting; an Opus 4.6 build ran 3 h 50 min ([harness design, 2026-03-24](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/harness-design-long-running-apps); [managed agents, 2026-04-08](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents)). OpenAI's Codex models compact repeatedly until the task is done, with tasks over 24 h ([GPT-5.1-Codex-Max](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-1-codex-max/); a GPT-5.3-Codex run of ~25 h and ~13M tokens, [blog](https://developers.openai.com/blog/run-long-horizon-tasks-with-codex)). Amp removed its handoff feature on 2026-05-06 in favor of automatic compaction at 90 % ([ampcode.com/news.rss](https://ampcode.com/news.rss)). None of these gives the number of compactions or a success rate.
- Our logs: 36 distinct compactions in 10 sessions (185 session files, 28,025 turns). Tool errors before vs after each boundary, within the same session: +0.14 points (10 turns, 95 % CI 0 to 0.42), +0.50 (30 turns, -0.18 to 1.57), +0.56 (100 turns, -0.16 to 1.80). Corrections and rejections, adjudicated by hand (28 in 6 compacted lanes): -1.67, -0.48, -0.06 points, every interval spanning 0. No trend with the compaction's order; the 8th and later have one session each. The longest sessions (E7: 46.6 h, 11 compactions; ETH: 82.4 h, 8; the lead: 85.7 h, 7) show no rise. One repeat across a boundary (a refused `create_goal` tried again) came with the agent saying it remembered the refusal. This is no measured harm, not proof of safety: the sample is small.

**What a rollover costs.**
- A takeover from notes needs more rediscovery than one from the raw trace: on 75 SWE-bench Verified tasks (181 checkpoints, 2,172 takeovers, three small open models) structured notes solved 50.8 / 43.6 / 44.8 % against 52.5 / 49.2 / 49.2 % with the raw trace, and needed 55 / 39 / 125 median agent events against 41 / 21 / 73 ([Handoff Debt, arxiv 2606.02875](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02875)).
- A searchable full history recovers what a summary drops: 32.1 % vs 92.5 % (GPT-4, [MemGPT Table 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.08560)); one search per question lifts a 400-word capsule from 37.5 % to 68.3 % (seven real sessions, [Hermes/Jev scorecard](https://github.com/kerpopule/hermes-jev-skills/blob/main/evals/compaction/results/SCORECARD-2026-09-20.md)).
- Practitioners on 2026 models report the next session repeating exploration after a clear ([HN 49900828](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49900828)) and plans losing the alternatives already rejected ([HN 47375137](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47375137)); a Codex user ran one session for ~15B cumulative tokens and calls cold resets harmful ([openai/codex #19278](https://github.com/openai/codex/issues/19278)). Anecdotes.
- Our logs: a fresh lane takes a median of 6 turns and 1.4 min (82 lanes) to its first productive action. In the first scratch handover the new session restarted a chain while the old copy still ran (fixed: the note says not to, and the new session waits for `HANDOVER_CHAINS_ENDED`). The second note lost a detached job an earlier session had started (fixed: jobs an earlier handover listed are carried).
- Opus 5.5's thinking blocks are tied to the conversation that produced them ([docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/opus-5-5/whats-new-opus-5-5#thinking-blocks-are-tied-to-the-model-that-produced-them)): a note never carries everything.

**Where a fresh start wins.** When the work is stuck: restarting with the lessons of the failed attempts beat continuing on 82 MettleBench tasks (78.0 vs 62.2 %, GPT-5.4), though continuing won on Terminal-Bench (78.7 vs 70.8 %) ([AgentRewind, arxiv 2608.14380](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.14380v1)); restarting after about the 3rd failed debugging attempt helped all 6 models tested ([Scientific Reports 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-27846-5)). And for a new task: Anthropic's guidance for 1M Claude Code is to keep one session for one load-bearing task and clear for a genuinely new one ([session management](https://claude.com/resources/articles/using-claude-code-session-management-and-1m-context)). A Dori lane is one task by design, so this case comes up rarely.

**What compaction loses, and how to cut it.** Constraints first: rules on how the work must be done survive generic summaries 17 % of the time ([Lost in Compaction](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.11242v1)); compacting non-system rules raised forbidden tool calls from 0 to 30 % while system-message rules did not decay ([Governance Decay](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.22528v2), Sonnet 4.6 and GPT-5.4-mini). Anthropic saw stated beliefs shift after compactions in 10-34 h runs ([incident report](https://www.anthropic.com/research/alignment-assessment-cybersecurity-incidents)). This argues for keeping constraints outside the summary (the lane's brief and the owner's words on file), not for rolling over.

**Recommendation: rollover off by default, proposed only after the cheap steps failed** (then: at the 3rd same failed fix; since 2026-10-12: rung 4 of the stuck ladder). Compaction only is the normal mode, with continuity checks rather than a reset clock: a forgotten constraint can produce a wrong action that succeeds on the first try, so the same-fix count never fires for it. When a lane confirmably breaks one of the owner's standing constraints, contradicts the verified state of its work, or cannot say what its goal and next action are (an owner-check finding, the context rung; a `[LEAD] not done` for a forgotten rule), first stop that action and have it re-read its brief and the owner's words (`dori owner-check <key> --reread`, after the lead confirmed the finding), then verify; propose a rollover (`dori rollover-propose <key>`) only when the drift comes back after that (rung 4). That trigger stays with the lead, by hand: there is no measured count for it. "No rollover at all" would give up the one case with controlled evidence for a fresh start, and the owner's manual move when a session goes wrong; both stay behind the owner's yes. When a rollover happens, the mechanisms below make it lose as little as the evidence says is possible.

## Upsides kept, downsides removed

A fresh session's upsides are a clean context and a way out of a repeated mistake; it keeps them because nothing of the old conversation comes along except what the note carries. Each downside has a guard and a test (`L` = `scripts/test/lane-handover.test.ts`, `H` = `scripts/test/handover.test.ts`):

| Downside | Guard | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Tacit context is lost: decisions, the owner's exact words, rejected approaches | The old session's own model writes the conversation summary at its safe point, under five checked headings, the owner's words and constraints quoted exactly; `dori handover ready` refuses without them | L "dori handover ready runs only in the lane's own pane..." |
| The summary still drops something | The note names the old session's full log (it stays on disk) and tells the new session to search it before asking; the fix attempts of the old session are listed from the registry, not from the summary | L "moves the lane...", L "a tried fix the restatement leaves out is filled in..." |
| The new session misunderstands and acts on it | Before it is committed it writes a restatement (Goal, Owner's words, Decisions, Next step, Ruled out, Questions). It is checked against the recorded fix attempts and the lane's goal (both kept apart from the summary) and against the owner's quoted words and the decisions in the summary. What it left out is filled in, not rolled back: a supplement file names each item, its source and the old log lines behind it (`HANDOVER_SUPPLEMENT`), and the new session restates and acks again. Only what is still missing after `handover.supplementRounds` (2) rounds rolls the move back: a session that does not take in what it is given is the real warning sign. Rounds and items go to `rollover-log.jsonl`. Its questions, only what neither the note nor the old log answers, go to the lead (`HANDOVER_QUESTIONS`) | L "a tried fix the restatement leaves out is filled in...", L "the owner's words and decisions the restatement leaves out...", L "a restatement that still leaves out a tried fix after the supplement rounds...", L "a new session that does not answer a supplement...", L "no restatement means the check fails..." |
| Re-orientation cost | The note puts everything in one place (brief, goal, Done, todos, monitors, chains, jobs, reports, summary); the new session reads it once | L "the note carries brief, goal, Done..." |
| Monitors stop | The new session re-arms each monitor; the handover checks command and filter (not just the name) in the new manifest before closing the old one | L "rolls back when a monitor is not re-armed...", H "a monitor counts as re-armed only with the same command and filter..." |
| Work stops: a chain dies with the old session | Refused while an unlisted chain runs (the agent's children, its eval kernel's children and jobs started from the pane's shell, with their children) or a background eval cell still runs; the session's own launcher shell and idle kernel are not chains; an automatic start never moves a lane that runs or watches a training or eval chain; detached jobs, also those an earlier handover listed, are never touched | L "refuses while a local chain would die...", L "the lane's own launcher shell and an idle eval kernel...", L "work under the lane's own processes still refuses...", L "a background eval cell that ended...", L "an automatic rollover never moves a lane that watches a training chain...", L "a job started from the pane's shell...", L "a job an earlier handover listed..." |
| Double execution: two copies of one chain | The note says not to restart chains; the new session gets `HANDOVER_CHAINS_ENDED` only after the close, when every pid of the chain (children included) is gone, else `HANDOVER_CHAIN_SURVIVED` | L "moves the lane..." (no double run), L "a chain whose parent ended but whose child still runs..." |
| Double execution: the new session starts working before the move is final | Its first message has no work keyword; it only arms monitors and answers the probe until `[LEAD] HANDOVER_COMMITTED`, sent after the old session closed | L "moves the lane..." (commit after close), H "moves config, env, lanes and cards..." |
| Double replies: both sessions talk | The old agent process is frozen (SIGSTOP) while both live and resumed only to roll back or to be closed; a quiet mark makes `dori send`/`ask`/`thread` refuse from its pane, kept with `--keep-old` or when the close fails | L "rolls back when the new session runs at another thinking level..." (frozen, then thawed), L "--keep-old leaves the old session open...", H "the old lead is quiet..." |
| The old session dies during the checks | The new one stays the lane instead of moving back to a dead pane (`HANDOVER_OLD_GONE`) | L "when the old session ends during the checks..." |
| The move breaks halfway | Pending journal, repair on the next run, rollback on any failed check (see Failure modes) | L "a run killed midway...", L "an error in the middle..." |

What this gives is verified operational resumability, not a lossless move: the summary and the restatement come from the same kind of model and can share a blind spot, only the tried fixes and the goal are checked against records kept apart from the summary (the owner's words and the decisions are checked against the summary itself), and Opus 5.5's thinking blocks never travel in a note. The old log on disk is the fallback for anything else.

Live run on a scratch lane (2026-10-11, `/tmp/lho-qa/t2-*`): the lane wrote its summary under the five headings and marked ready; the move froze the old agent (`HANDOVER_FROZEN`), the new session restated goal, next step and the ruled-out fix with the owner's constraint quoted, the old session was closed and only then the new one committed (`HANDOVER_COMMITTED`, then `HANDOVER_CHAINS_ENDED`); in 34 two-second samples the chain never ran twice, and the detached job stayed alive. A second live run (`/tmp/lho-qa/t3-*`) tested the supplement: told by its brief to leave Ruled out empty, the new session's first restatement missed the tried fix and six of the owner's quoted lines; one supplement round listed the seven items with the old log lines behind each, the new session added them all, and the move committed after 1 round (`HANDOVER_SUPPLEMENT_OK`, `supplementRounds: 1` in the log). It also showed a quote holding escaped quotes being split in two, fixed since.

The lead's own handover has the commit gate, the quiet mark, the summary headings and the restatement with supplements (since 2026-10-16, owner: "자동 교체 시 맥락 손실은 없지?"), and the freeze at the commit (since 2026-10-16, owner: "교체가 완료되면 얼려줘"). Unlike a lane, the old lead is not stopped while the checks run, only told to stay quiet with its `dori send`/`ask`/`thread` refused: it must take over again at once if the move rolls back. Once the move is committed its agent and every process under it get SIGSTOP (`HANDOVER_OLD_FROZEN`), so it spends no tokens and does nothing; a rolled-back move stops nothing. The frozen session keeps its pane and its log (JSONL, which the next handover's supplements and anyone looking back read), is skipped by `sync` and `dead-panes`, and is closed `handover.frozenCloseH` (24) hours later by `freshness` (resumed first, then `aoe session stop` and `aoe rm`; the log stays). `dori handover thaw <pane>` resumes one frozen by mistake, `dori handover close-frozen [<pane>]` closes one at once, `dori handover frozen` lists them. The owner's report of the rollover gets the `handover.frozenText` line (H "moves config, env, lanes and cards... and only then freezes the old one", H "an old lead that cannot be frozen...", H "an inbox probe acked by the old lead does not count..." (no signal on a rollback), `scripts/test/frozen.test.ts`).

### The lead's restatement

The new lead writes what it understood under Goal, Owner's words, Open lanes, Open work, Open cards, Timers, Promised follow-ups, Decisions, Next step and Questions, after both probes and before the old lead is closed. Checked against records kept apart from any summary: every open lane key (the registry), every open card id waiting for the owner, every timer's unit, each todo and the goal of the old session, and each of the owner's messages of the last 24 hours in the inbox (so an automatic rollover, which has no summary, still carries what he said last); and against the old lead's summary (`--note`, headings from `dori handover --template`): its quoted owner's words, decisions and promised follow-ups. An open lane, card or timer counts only under its own heading. What is left out comes back as a supplement with the old log lines behind it, up to `handover.supplementRounds` rounds; after that the move rolls back and the old lead stays (H "the new lead restates what it understood...", H "a new lead whose restatement still leaves out an open lane...", H "a new lead that never writes its restatement...", H "the owner's recent messages and the old lead's summary...", H "an old lead's summary without its headings...").

What the two lane supplements so far filled in (`rollover-log.jsonl`): hr35-e7 on 10-13, two tried fixes and one decision; cyto-memory-upgrade on 10-15, two of the owner's constraints ("NEVER experiment on the live node ...", "모델 이름 코드에 박지 말 것"). Both were things only the old conversation knew and the new session's own summary-reading skipped: rules on how the work must be done and failed attempts, the two kinds published work also finds lost first. Nothing a record holds (the goal, open items) was missed.

## After the commit: the audit ledger

The checks above stop at what the handover's own records and the old session's summary hold. What the old conversation alone held (the owner's words the lead relayed mid-work, the lead's latest instructions) and what slips after the commit (a todo or the goal not rebuilt, a monitor not armed) were not measured. The owner, 2026-10-19 19:45 KST: "일단 이렇게 해두고 롤오버 하는 경우 있을 때마다 누락되는 경우나 아니면 롤오버 기능의 개선을 하기 위한 장치 같은 걸 하나 해두면 좋을 것 같아". So every committed handover, lane or lead, is audited once after it (`dori handover audit`, started by `freshness` `handover.auditAfterMin` minutes after the commit; [scripts.md](scripts.md) has the details):

- **Compared**: the old session's log (it stays on disk; the old lead is only frozen): the owner's words the lead relayed (counted as his only with proof: his message id in the inbox, or the card he answered; any other quote is the lead's words) and the lead's latest instructions, against what reached the new session: its brief, the handover note, its restatement, the `.md` files those name, the cwd's `AGENTS.md`, the directive ledger; the old session's open todos, goal and monitors against the new session's own; for a lane, the summary's decisions and tried fixes against the restatement as it is now.
- **Sorted**: an item only the conversation held is a gap only when a reviewer model calls it STANDING (still holds, with a risk HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW); what the new session already holds in other words (CARRIED: the reviewer reads its restatement, brief and the ledger's rules), a request already carried out (DONE) and questions or status talk (CHATTER) are counted, not recorded. Measured on the first two handovers it ran on (2026-10-19): 62 of the owner's quotes in the old conversations reached the new sessions nowhere, but by hand only 21 still held, 17 of those reached them through the directive ledger, the plan file or the cwd's `AGENTS.md`, and 4 were really lost; one of them (the account names decided for a queued card) mattered.
- **Recorded**: one line per gap in `<stateDir>/handover/audit.jsonl` (lane, time, handover, stage, kind, a short summary with secret values masked, source, fixed), plus what the supplement rounds before the commit filled in (stage `supplement`) and what a session later reports it did not know (`dori handover gap <key> --kind K --text T`, stage `reported`; both notes tell the new session about it). Nothing left out: no line.
- **Filled in at once**: HIGH and MEDIUM gaps, and every todo, goal and monitor gap, go to the new session once through the supplement path (`HANDOVER_SUPPLEMENT`, `<stamp>.audit-supplement-1.md`), with the request to write what only the conversation held into its `AGENTS.md` or memory, so the next handover finds it in a file; the check after its ack sets `fixed`. LOW ones are recorded only.
- **Fed back**: each Monday after 08:30 (`handover.auditSummaryAt`, `scorecard.timeZone`) the lead gets one `kind:"rollover-audit"` row in its inbox: handovers, gaps by stage, fixed or not, the kinds and lanes that lose most (`dori handover audit-summary`). That is the list of what the handover should carry next; it never goes to the owner's channel. The first one (2026-10-19: 10 gaps, 7 of them the owner's words) led to the note's section "The owner's messages since the last handover".

Nothing in it weakens the checks before the commit (the restatement, the supplement rounds, the rollback), and it does not change which lanes move (training lanes stay with the lead).

## What a lane handover carries and what it refuses

The note (`<stateDir>/handover/lanes/<key>-<stamp>.md`, plus `.json`) holds: the brief path, owner goal and Done, open todos and goal of the old session, every monitor it runs (persistent or not; the new session re-arms each as persistent), its local chains, its detached jobs (and those an earlier handover listed that still run), its last report, direction and history, the work thread, model and thinking level, the conversation summary the old session wrote (`dori handover ready --note`, plus the lead's `--note`), the fix attempts of the old session, the path of the old session's full log, the approval ref, and the owner's own messages since the lane's last handover (inbox rows written by his account, in the lane's thread or relayed into the old conversation by message id, with the id and link; at most 40, newest kept; what the lead or a bot wrote is left out).

The conversation summary has five headings, which `dori handover ready` checks: **Owner's words and constraints** (quoted exactly, how the work must be done included; summaries lose these first), **Decisions and why**, **Ruled out** (with the result that ruled each out), **In progress** (files, commands and tests with results, verified apart from attempted), **Next step**. The old session's own model writes it, so it comes from the full conversation, not from a compacted one. The owner's words go in double quotes: the new session's restatement is checked for each quote, and a quote it leaves out comes back to it with the old log line that holds it.

- **Local chain**: a child process of the lane's omo agent, of its eval kernel, or of the pane's shell (and the launcher shell between it and the agent), that is neither omo's own helper (MCP servers, the subagent RPC host, the provider CLI), a monitor, nor a zombie (state `Z` in `ps`, `<defunct>`: it already ended), counted with every live process under it. The session itself is not one: the launcher shell aoe starts the agent from (`/bin/bash /dev/fd/4`) and omo's eval kernel (`python3 -u .../kernels/py/prelude.py`) end with any move, so counting them refused every automatic move of a lane that once used python eval (yoodori-gate-gaps, 6 refusals and a give-up, 2026-10-18/19). It ends when the old session closes. The handover refuses while one runs, unless the lane listed it in `dori handover ready` (run in its own pane, valid `handover.readyMin` minutes, covering only the pids it listed then) or `--force` is given.
- **Detached job**: a process outside the pane's process tree whose environment still names the lane's omo session (`PI_SESSION_ID`) or aoe session. It survives, is listed, and is never touched. `HANDOVER_DETACHED_ALIVE n/m` after the close says how many still run.
- **Background eval cell**: a detached eval cell (python in the kernel, js in the agent) has no process of its own, so it is read from the session log: a start (`Eval cell <id> detached and is running`) without its end since the agent started refuses (`N eval cell(s) still run in the background: <ids>`), unless the lane's `dori handover ready` came after the cell started, or `--force`. What a cell started as a process is a local chain under the kernel.
- A running turn (`esc to interrupt`) or an open question in the pane also refuses, even after `dori handover ready` (it covers chains only: a nudge or a message can start a turn after it).
- It refuses when run from the lane's own pane: the lead runs it.

## Failure modes

Each row is a point where a rollover can break, what is left, how it is put right, and the test that holds it (`scripts/test/lane-handover.test.ts` = L, `scripts/test/handover.test.ts` = H).

| Where it breaks | State left | How it is put right | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before anything opens: no approval, an unanswered or "no" card, a live chain, a running turn, an open question, run from the lane's pane, no live aoe session | nothing changed | `HANDOVER_REFUSED`, exit 2; a refused real attempt is logged | L "refuses while a local chain...", L "refuses without the owner's approval", L "an approval is...", H "refuses without the owner's approval" |
| `aoe add` fails | nothing changed | `HANDOVER_FAIL`, exit 3 | (aoe error path, same as the lead's) |
| The new session does not start: usage limit or quota exhausted, crash, no prompt in 3 min | a new session that is not working | it is stopped and trashed, the old one is told it stays, exit 3 | L "a new session that does not start is closed" |
| The first message does not register | same | same | (sendVerified path) |
| The new session runs at another thinking level or model (omo can start at medium) | lane already pointed at the new pane | lane and cards back to the old pane, the new session closed, the old told, exit 3 | L "rolls back when the new session runs at another thinking level" |
| A monitor is not re-armed in time | same | same | L "rolls back when a monitor is not re-armed..." |
| The probe is not acked, or acked from the old pane | same | same | L same test (acked from another pane) |
| An exception in the middle (a disk, a tool or a parse error) | lane may point at either pane | caught: the same repair as a killed run | L "an error in the middle of the move is repaired" |
| The handover process is killed or the host reboots midway | `<stateDir>/handover/lanes/<key>.pending.json` left behind; both sessions may live | the next `dori handover --lane <key>` repairs first (`HANDOVER_INTERRUPTED`, `HANDOVER_RECOVERED`): old session alive -> lane back on it, new one closed; only the new one alive -> lane stays on it; neither alive (reboot) -> registry left for `dori sync` / dead-panes. A journal whose process still lives refuses a second run | L "a run killed midway...", L "a killed run whose old session is already gone..." |
| Two handovers of one lane, or of the lead, at once | - | a lock per lane (`<key>.lock`) and one for the lead; a lock whose process is gone is taken over | H "refuses while another handover holds the lock" |
| Both sessions alive during the checks: double replies | the old session could still answer the owner | the old one is told to stop, and a quiet mark makes `dori send`, `dori ask` and `dori thread` refuse from its pane (`HANDOVER_QUIET`, exit 4) until the run ends. The mark expires `verifyMin + 10` minutes after it was set, so a crashed run cannot silence a pane for good. With `--keep-old` it stays until it expires | L "the old lane is quiet while it is replaced...", H "the old lead is quiet from the moment the new lead takes over..." |
| A report arrives twice (old and new session) during the checks | two `[REPORT]` lines for one lane | harmless: freshness keeps the latest report per lane; the old one is told to stop first | (by design) |
| Closing the old session fails | the lane points at the new pane, the old idle | `HANDOVER_CLOSE_FAIL`, exit 1; close it with `aoe rm` | (closeAoeSession path) |
| The old lead cannot be frozen at the commit (no agent found under its pane, the signal refused) | the new lead is the lead; the old still runs | `HANDOVER_FREEZE_FAIL`, whatever was stopped is resumed and the old session is closed as before (`--keep-old`: left open, told it is no longer the lead) | H "an old lead that cannot be frozen..." |
| The old lead was frozen by mistake | its agent stopped, pane and log kept | `dori handover thaw <pane>` resumes it (children first, then the agent) and drops the record; it is still not the lead | `scripts/test/frozen.test.ts` |
| A frozen old lead is never closed (freshness down) | a stopped session holding memory | `dori handover frozen` lists it with its close time; `dori handover close-frozen` closes every due one | `scripts/test/frozen.test.ts` |
| An owner message arrives during the switch | the listener routes by the registry | before the repoint (seconds) it reaches the old pane, which keeps it if the handover rolls back; after it, the new pane | (by design) |
| A ready mark older than `readyMin`, or from another pane | - | ignored (`HANDOVER_READY_STALE`) | L "a ready mark covers only..." |
| The lane marks ready from omo's eval kernel, which stays alive while it idles | the kernel would count as a live chain and block the move | the ready mark also covers the process that ran `dori handover ready` | L "dori handover ready runs only in the lane's own pane and also covers..." |
| The new session starts a local chain again while the old copy still runs (the first live try did this) | the same work twice | the note says not to start them; after the close the handover checks the pids: all ended -> `[LEAD] HANDOVER_CHAINS_ENDED` (restart from checkpoints, skip finished steps); one still alive -> `HANDOVER_CHAIN_SURVIVED` (do not restart). Live re-check: never two copies in 84 samples | L "moves the lane..." (no double run), L "a chain that outlives the close is not restarted..." |
| The account pool gives the new session another account | - | `HANDOVER_ACCOUNT` line; the rollover log keeps both accounts | L "a chain that outlives the close... and a new account is logged" |
